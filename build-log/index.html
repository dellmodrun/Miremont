What a receipt proves.

A receipt is not a screenshot or an internal database row. It is the public connection between funding, work, verification and settlement.

Origin

Burn transaction

Builder wallet, mint, token amount, signature and Cursor account.

Work

Output commitment

Task definition, role, content hash, submission time and verification rule.

Settlement

Final outcome

Verdict, contribution points, reward epoch and SOL claim signature.

Nothing important stays off the record.

Each row expands into the complete receipt. Visitors can trace the burn, deadline, artifact, tests, reviewer quorum, challenge status, points and final SOL claim.

Task contract

Rules before the burn

Base points, difficulty, checks, output destination and expiry are committed before the builder signs.

Verification trail

Every verdict attributable

Test results and reviewer wallets are recorded. Self-review, duplicate outputs and late submissions are visibly invalid.

Money trail

Deposit to claim

Fee Router deposit, epoch snapshot, allocation pool and claim signature connect rewards to real received SOL.

Explorer-ready by design.

Once deployed, every receipt row will link to the relevant Solana transaction and program account. Until then, no placeholder hashes are presented as real activity.
