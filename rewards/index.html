Builder pool

70% by accepted points.

Only finalized receipts in the current 24-hour epoch participate. A builder receives the same fraction of this pool as their fraction of accepted points.

Reviewer pool

15% by recorded reviews.

Each of the three distinct reviewer accounts receives one unit. Self-review is rejected on-chain; reviewer governance remains an application-layer policy in v0.1.

The formula stays visible.

No discretionary allocation happens after an epoch closes. Anyone can recompute a claim from public receipt points and the recorded vault balance.

builder_pool = epoch_net_sol x 0.70
builder_share = builder_points
              / total_accepted_points
claimable_sol = builder_pool x builder_share

reviewer_pool = epoch_net_sol x 0.15
reviewer_claim = reviewer_pool
               / eligible_review_units
Hard rule

Rejected work receives zero.

Burning creates the right to attempt one task. It does not create yield or guarantee a reward. Empty pools roll forward; claims remain available for 90 days.

Settlement has no hidden step.

An epoch closes every 24 hours, but receipts enter settlement only after their challenge window ends. Vault balance, total points and claims can be recomputed from public accounts.
